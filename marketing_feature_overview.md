# Aurora Skylines: Feature Overview

## What the game is
Aurora Skylines is a premium, browser-based casual city builder and management simulation game. The player's main goal is to strategically construct and expand a thriving metropolis from a blank grid, maximizing the population while balancing a healthy financial budget and maintaining 100% energy coverage across the city. 

## Core gameplay loop
A typical play session revolves around a satisfying cycle of expansion and resource balancing:
1. **Infrastructure:** The player starts by laying down a network of roads to connect different sectors of their grid.
2. **Zoning:** Players zone residential areas to grow the population, commercial and industrial areas to generate income, and build services like parks to boost citizen happiness.
3. **Resource Management:** As the city grows, the player must monitor their budget and ensure enough power plants are built to meet the rising energy demands, as energy deficits heavily penalize income.
4. **Progression:** The player reinvests their profits into city-wide permanent upgrades, unlocking more efficiency and allowing for even greater expansion.

## Key features
*   **Grid-Based Building System:** A flexible 32x32 tile world where players can place and instantly demolish various structures, with buildings automatically rotating to face the nearest road.
*   **Dynamic Resource Economy:** An intricate balance of Gold, Population, Happiness, and Energy. Citizen happiness acts as an income multiplier, while energy shortages actively choke the city's revenue stream.
*   **City Upgrades Shop:** A progression system featuring 6 unique, cumulative upgrade tiers (such as Efficient Paving or Transit AI) that provide permanent bonuses to the city's economy and infrastructure.
*   **NPC Traffic AI:** A living city simulation featuring real-time vehicle traffic. Cars utilize a Breadth-First Search pathfinding algorithm to dynamically navigate the road networks the player builds.
*   **Day/Night Cycle:** A persistent 15-minute chronological cycle featuring smooth sky color transitions, sun and moon orbits, and dynamic window and street lamp glowing effects at night.
*   **Seamless Cloud Saving:** A robust 6-slot cloud architecture that allows players to save their progress online and seamlessly resume their city from any device.
*   **Glassmorphism UI:** A sleek, translucent heads-up display and categorized build menu that provides real-time statistics without cluttering the gameplay view.

## What makes it unique
Unlike many clunky or heavily menu-driven browser games, Aurora Skylines offers a seamless, native-feeling 3D experience right in the browser with zero loading screens. It uses procedurally generated 3D models and particles instead of heavy external assets, resulting in lightning-fast performance. Combined with its real-time traffic AI, dynamic day/night lighting, and robust cloud-save architecture, it provides a "premium game" feel rarely seen in web-based city builders.

## Tone & visual style
The game features a modern, premium, and clean aesthetic with an isometric, orthographic camera perspective. The visual style leans towards a vibrant and stylized "glassmorphism" look, using rich colors, smooth lighting gradients, procedural particles (like fireflies and stars), and translucent UI elements. The tone is relaxing yet strategic, supported by a continuous ambient synth soundtrack and procedural sound effects.

## Target audience
Aurora Skylines is designed for casual gamers and strategy fans who enjoy management simulations, aesthetic progression, and relaxing gameplay loops. It appeals to players who want the depth of a city builder without the overwhelming complexity of hardcore simulators, all accessible instantly through their web browser.
