## 🌟 About the Game

**Elmiral** is an indie 2D pixel-art fantasy action RPG built with Godot. Descend into atmospheric dungeons, master class-specific combat abilities, collect rare loot, and battle formidable bosses in a handcrafted fantasy world.

> 📝 **Showcase Repository**  
> *This repository serves as a public project showcase and hub to follow development updates. The main game source code is private.*

---

- **3 Unique Dungeons** with distinctive boss fights

## 🗺️ The World of Elmiral

* **🏰 Town of the Last Lantern**  
  Your primary hub for gear, preparation, rest, and organizing your next run.

* **💀 Forgotten Crypt**  
  Descend into ancient, crumbling halls and face the relentless **Crypt Warden**.

* **🌿 Forest Ruins**  
  Navigate overgrown, trap-laden paths and confront the massive **Mire Ogre**.

* **🏛️ Ancient Temple**  
  Explore flooded, submerged chambers with unique environmental hazards and defeat the **Sunken Colossus**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- ⚔️ **Real-time Action Combat:** Fluid movement, active dodging, and skill-based combat sequences.
- 🎒 **Loot & Progression:** Collect gear, manage inventory, and optimize stats across multiple difficulty tiers.
- 🎨 **Atmospheric Pixel Art:** Hand-crafted sprites, fluid animation, and immersive retro visuals.
- 🔄 **Ongoing Evolution:** Active polish across dynamic UI, sound design, balance, and boss mechanics.
